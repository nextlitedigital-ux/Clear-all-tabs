Privacy Policy for Clear All Tabs

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for choosing Clear All Tabs. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our browser extension.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Clear All Tabs does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user activity.

  • We do not record browsing history, URLs, search queries, or page contents.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or credentials.
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.

2. Permissions

  • contextMenus: Used only to add quick tab-closing options and a Settings link to the right-click menu.
  • storage: Used only to save your preferences (closing behavior, custom URL, pinned tab setting) in your browser's extension storage.

All processing occurs inside your browser. We do not collect or receive any data.

3. Changes to This Policy

Any updates will be published on this page with an updated date.
